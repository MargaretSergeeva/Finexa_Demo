1. Risk Taxonomy Domains

Delivery (Scope / Schedule / Cost)

LLM reasoning errors, vector matching inaccuracies, schema breaks, wrapper API timeouts, and text-to-SQL injections.

Operational / Process

Reviewer gate latency, spreadsheet regression, operational support escalation burial, and workflow friction.

Stakeholder / Change / Adoption

Executive alignment, loss of PM decision authority, and cross-functional trust breakdown.
